=== Mismatch analysis (unchanged) ===
The expectation VERIFIED_UNCHANGED for the synthetic fixture assumes the whole
registry is representable by that fixture. It is not: 23 of 27 patch points have
no matching surface there, they probe as MISS, and the runner treats an
incomplete probe as a refusal. Reaching VERIFIED_UNCHANGED therefore requires a
fixture bundle that covers every required patch point, which the repository's
synthetic fixtures do not and cannot provide at their current size. This is a
coverage gap in the expectation and in the fixtures, not a measured product
defect: the runner behaved as designed on both runs.
